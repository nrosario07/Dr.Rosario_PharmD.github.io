# ClinicalRx Roadmap

## Phase 0: Clean foundation
Static site, navigation, profile, privacy, CI, Pages deployment and smoke tests.

## Phase 1: Profile migration
Verified public CV, experience, research, publications, teaching, presentations and projects.

## Phase 2: Drug Intelligence
Deterministic drug identity and source-backed reference first. Add permitted APIs only after current terms, payload, error handling and provenance are documented.

## Phase 3: Medication Review/Reconciliation
Separate case state; medication history, sources, discrepancies, intentional/unintentional classification, reconciliation actions, follow-up and output review gates. No persistence or network transmission of case details.

## Phase 4: Guideline Repository
Cross-specialty taxonomy, historical versions, issuing body, publication/update dates, six-month banner and review workflow.

## Phase 5: Professional Practice
Texas CPA and pharmacist practice legal reference library, primary literature and white papers.

## Phase 6: Billing Intelligence
Texas-first and national CMS-ready structure; retain CMS PFS source material with provenance and effective-date controls.

## Phase 7: Source governance automation
Monitoring → retrieval → validation → human approval → publication. Do not skip stages.

## Phase 8: Independent acceptance
Clinical, legal, coding, privacy, accessibility and deployed-browser review.
