# ClinicalRx evidence review — v0.9.0

## Supplied documents used

Read the full supplied MTP guidance PDF and workbook category rows; 36 distinct rationales in four domains are included as labels with original documentation prompts. The source describes individualized MTP identification, separate recommendations/resolution and CMM care process. No patient records or original copyrighted full documents are published.

The ASHP documentation guideline (2003;60:705-707) supports recording actual care, rationale, recommendations, actions and response. The supplied Novitas instructions were text-read and page 4 visually inspected. Its own footnote distinguishes current office/outpatient MDM/time selection from historical history/exam grids. Historical 99201 and old average times are not activated. The site separates documentation comparison from eligibility and never manufactures extra history/exam or countable time.

Rosario’s supplied publication: 95 adults; 48/61 reporting 9–10 versus 16/34 reporting ≤8 had MPR≥0.80. Below 9 flags investigation rather than proving nonadherence; 18/34 (53%) below 9 had MPR<0.80. The publication’s correlation/fair agreement and no ingestion gold standard limit individual prediction. The supplied 2016 P187 pilot abstract explicitly says work in progress/anticipated results; no completed MMAS-8 correlation was supplied. MMAS-8 items are not reproduced.

## Economic amount boundary

Each MTP displays USD357.62–700 as a range of two reported overall intervention-study means (Hasham/Lee), with AMA citations, settings and unknown/noncurrent price-year limits. This is a broad analogy, not a type-specific validated avoidance coefficient, uncertainty interval, pooled meta-analysis, observed saving or revenue. No additive MTP total is calculated. Requests for validated per-rationale estimates remain incomplete until matching study tables, outcomes, causal probabilities and price basis can be verified; this release does not fabricate them. Existing local risk-difference/direct-cost scenarios remain available separately.

Potential quality matches from ICD codes are review prompts, not eligibility, gap closure or clinical target personalization. Current authorized specification review remains necessary.

Switch schedules are a bounded pilot: adult MDD sertraline↔escitalopram with clinician-verified dose, and stable adult schizophrenia olanzapine→risperidone under narrow constraints. UK/Australian guidance is attributed and requires US-label/institution review.
