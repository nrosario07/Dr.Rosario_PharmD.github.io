# Future monetization — planning only
Reviewed October 25, 2026. No affiliate account, tracking, authentication or purchases are added.

Amazon Associates rates vary by category. The US standard schedule lists Health & Personal Care at 1%, while selected categories such as Luxury Beauty may qualify for 10%; do not model all nutrition purchases at 10%.
https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/node/topic/GRXPHT8U84RAYDXZ

KDP eBooks have 35% or eligible 70% royalty options, with territory, pricing and delivery/tax conditions. Paperback royalties are 50% or 60% depending on price/market, minus printing costs. These are not guaranteed net profit margins.
https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G200644210
https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G201834330

Before implementation: review content rights and AI disclosure requirements, independent clinical/editorial review, current category rates, clear affiliate disclosures, accessible privacy/terms, and a model including refunds, printing/delivery costs and taxes. Avoid implying supplements are personalized treatment or that commercial placement changes clinical recommendations. Core platform acceptance takes priority.
